Featured Expedition: 7-Day Northern Wonderland Tour

Our signature 7-day tour takes travelers deep into the heart of Gilgit-Baltistan. The journey begins with a scenic drive through the Karakoram Highway, followed by stopovers at Rakaposhi View Point, the historical Altit and Baltit Forts, and the serene turquoise waters of Attabad Lake.

All transport is handled in climate-controlled 4x4 off-road vehicles with certified mountain drivers. Travelers receive daily buffet breakfasts, professional photography services, and bonfires at night under starlit mountain skies.

Travel Advisory & Packing Tip

Weather in the northern regions fluctuates rapidly between day and night. We strongly suggest carrying a windbreaker jacket, comfortable waterproof trekking boots, sunscreen, and essential personal medicines. National ID cards (CNIC/Passport) are mandatory at security checkpoints.
